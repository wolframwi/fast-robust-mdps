# inventory50

Source: Stylized inventory control benchmark from operations research.

Inventory levels 0..49 with order actions 0..4. Demand distribution over
0..5 is [0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.2, 0.15, 0.05]. Costs: order 0.5, holding 0.1,
shortage 1.0. Rewards are negative costs.

Initial distribution: deterministic at inventory level 0.
